# Voice and hook patterns observed in real SzroNox Rolki (VOICE-01 §C)

Status: **root analysis, 2026-10-11, for operator review.** Provisional. Nothing here is frozen or
used by any prompt until the operator approves it (prompt pack §9.1–9.2).

Source: DEV database, read-only transaction, 2026-10-11. 52 Reels (25 soft-deleted). There are 52
hooks in total: 20 written by the team, 32 generated by AI. 7 Reels have a selected hook, and 23
have a script. Only Reels the team **published** are quoted below; those are public. Unpublished
material is described only as patterns.

## 1. What the data says about the product

- **Titles are almost never typed:** 9 of 52 Reels have one, and only 1 of 6 published Reels.
  The list relies on derived labels, so naming has to be automatic (CAPTURE-DRAFT-01 Part A).
- **Selection is underused.** Published Reels often carry a team-written hook that was never
  selected. A "selected hook only" rule finds too few examples (VOICE-01 rule corrected in §5).
- **The team prefers its own hooks.** Of 7 selected hooks, 5 were written by the team. 2 AI hooks
  won, and both copy the team's pattern: a quoted claim + a challenge.
- **The ideas pile is used as a scratchpad:** 23 of 35 ideas were deleted. That's healthy, but it
  argues for zero-effort capture and an automatic draft.

## 2. Hooks the team writes

| Trait | Observation |
| --- | --- |
| Length | **4–9 words.** Never a full explanatory sentence. |
| Form | Mostly a **question** (about 3 in 4); otherwise a bare teaser statement. |
| Device 1 — the claim echoed back | The opponent's claim or framing, repeated as a question so it sounds absurd: `Austriacy wymyślili Ukraińców?`, `Wolność to przymusowy pobór?` (the latter unpublished, described). |
| Device 2 — a twisted familiar phrase | A known formula with one word swapped: `Nie zabijaj, nie cudzołóż, nie śmieć?` (the commandments + littering); slogan or stock-phrase subversion (`do pracy, czy do garów?`); game language for a politician (`odblokował nowy poziom`). |
| Device 3 — a stark list or contrast | `NATO bez Anglii, Francji i krajów bałtyckich?` — a concrete, surprising combination of names. |
| Device 4 — claim + challenge | `Wojsko jest bezradne - twierdzi Mentzen, jaka jest prawda?` (an AI hook the team selected). |
| Ellipsis for the twist | `…` before the punchline word. |
| Punctuation | A question mark or nothing. One `!` appears only in a selected AI hook. |

## 3. AI hooks the team did **not** choose

These are the patterns to steer away from:
- **Too long:** 10–18 words, reads like an article headline (`Dlaczego wojsko wezwało policję do
  drona przy bazie w Redzikowie?`).
- **Explainer questions** (`Dlaczego…`, `Czy … było w ogóle…`) instead of a provocation.
- **The colon-headline formula** (`Tajna odprawa w Pałacu Elizejskim: jak Macron…`).
- **A binary "X czy Y?" with loaded adjectives** (`cyniczna prowokacja czy pogarda dla służb?`).
- **Newsroom phrasing** (`Sprawdzamy sensacyjne nagłówki`, `co pokazał im Macron?`).
- **Generic stacking** (`Watykan, ekologia i... papież z przyszłości?`).

## 4. Spoken scripts (not captions)

- **Length:** 46–83 words (about 20–35 seconds).
- **Opening:** answers or continues the hook immediately, with no lead-in (`Na to pytanie może nam
  odpowiedzieć Francja.`, `To może Francuzi wymyślili Polaków?`).
- **Then:** who said what, attributed by name (`Mentzen stwierdził…`, `Putin… powiedział…`).
- **Then:** concrete rebuttal facts and numbers with a named source (Bloomberg, Deloitte, the
  distance from the base).
- **Ending — this is where the provisional SZRONOX-VOICE is wrong for speech.** Real scripts end
  in one of three ways:
  - a **verdict** on the opponent (`Czyli Konfederacja znowu kłamie.`);
  - a **Green alternative** (`Dlatego Zielone rozwiązanie jest jasne…`, `Tylko Zieloni…`);
  - **a question to the audience** (`Co o tym myślicie?`).
- **Register:** colloquial spoken Polish. The **first person** is allowed (`I ja nie mam z tym
  problemu`, `Trzymam kciuki`), as are emotional emphasis (`błagam`) and dry irony.
  First-person plural appears for shared stakes (`Nie możemy tego bagatelizować`).

**Consequence:** `SZRONOX-VOICE` was inferred from one *caption*. Its bans on first person,
audience questions, a moral ending and rallying plural contradict how the team actually speaks.
Proposal: split it into a **SPOKEN** voice (`develop` script, `generate_hooks`) based on §2–4,
and keep the current profile only for **captions** (`generate_publication`), corrected per §4a.

### 4a. Captions as published

- 2–3 factual sentences adding context, with the source named inline (`Według zestawienia
  Bloomberga cytowanego przez „Puls Biznesu”…`), or one ironic sentence.
- Hashtags: 5. `#Zieloni` first (capitalisation varies), then topic tags. The team **does** use
  generic tags like `#polityka` and `#ekologia`, so the provisional ban on generic tags doesn't
  match practice.

## 5. Proposed prompt material (provisional, for operator approval)

```
SZRONOX SPOKEN VOICE (hooks and scripts)

Hooks: 4–9 words. Prefer a question. Use one device:
 - repeat the opponent's claim or framing as a question so its absurdity shows;
 - twist a familiar phrase, slogan or formula by swapping one word;
 - a concrete surprising combination of names or facts;
 - "<claim> - twierdzi <who>, jaka jest prawda?".
Never: explainer questions starting "Dlaczego"/"Czy ... w ogóle", colon headlines, "X czy Y"
with loaded adjectives, newsroom phrases ("sprawdzamy", "co pokazał"), more than 10 words.

Script: 45–85 spoken words. Answer or continue the hook in the first sentence. Attribute the
claim by name. Rebut with 2–3 concrete facts or numbers and name their source. End with ONE of:
a one-line verdict on the claim, the Green alternative, or a short question to viewers.
Colloquial spoken Polish; first person allowed; dry irony allowed; no insults.
```
